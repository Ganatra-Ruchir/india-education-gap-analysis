What do India's classrooms actually tell us? 📚🇮🇳

I stopped looking at the national literacy average and started looking at the spread — state by state. Three things stood out:

📊 ~20 percentage points separate the top and bottom states
👧 The male–female literacy gap tops 11 points in some states
🏫 Crowded classrooms and school dropout move together (r ≈ 0.89)

That last one matters most. A high pupil-teacher ratio tracking with higher dropout isn't destiny — it's a policy lever. Hire teachers, shrink classes, keep kids in school.

This is a small open-source data project framed around UN SDG 4: Quality Education. Full code + charts on my GitHub (link in bio). Fork it, drop in your own data, and see what your state's numbers say.
