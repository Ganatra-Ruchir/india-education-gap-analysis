What do India's classrooms actually tell us? 📚🇮🇳

I stopped looking at the national literacy average and started looking at the spread — state by state, using real 2015-16 UDISE government data. Three things stood out:

📊 ~30 percentage points separate the top and bottom states (Kerala 93.9% vs. Bihar 63.8%)
👧 The male–female literacy gap hits 27.9 points in Rajasthan
🔗 Higher-literacy states have meaningfully smaller gender gaps (r ≈ -0.74) — the two move together

One honest twist: I also tested whether crowded classrooms track lower exam pass rates, expecting a clean story. The real data didn't support it (r ≈ -0.13, essentially no relationship) — so I reported that as a null result instead of reaching for a stronger claim the data doesn't back.

This is a small open-source data project framed around UN SDG 4: Quality Education. Full code + charts on my GitHub (link in bio). Fork it, drop in your own state's data, and see what it says.
